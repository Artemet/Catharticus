About

Company Overview

Catharticus is an elite, ANCEX-licensed European defense trade operator and strategic risk management firm. Headquartered in Romania, we orchestrate complex, multi-level procurement operations and technology integration for the global defense and security sectors.

We do not just supply; we secure capabilities. By combining unyielding regulatory compliance with deep intelligence-driven market insights, Catharticus bridges the gap between leading manufacturers and institutional entities worldwide. In an era of shifting global security dynamics, we deliver mission-critical solutions, flawless logistics, and strategic resilience under any operational conditions.

Services

Our Services

Military & Industrial Explosives

Global Supply of High-Yield Energetic Materials. Catharticus orchestrates the direct procurement and strategic supply of high-purity military and industrial explosives, including C4, RDX, TNT, and PETN. We command flawless, large-scale bulk logistics for defense industries worldwide, guaranteeing absolute compliance with international security protocols and rigorous quality standards under any operational conditions.

Ammunition & Ordnance

Precision Fires. Fused with Unrivaled Manufacturing Access. We deliver a comprehensive portfolio of artillery ammunition, mortar rounds, and tank ordnance, with an absolute focus on critical 122mm and 155mm calibers. Leveraging institutional access and direct, flexible contracts with premier manufacturers, we rapidly close complex procurement gaps, securing both high-volume standard configurations and highly specialized tactical solutions.

Unmanned Aerial Systems & Interceptors

Next-Generation UAS & Air Defense Interceptors. Operating in tight synergy with elite Ukrainian defense tech innovators and combat-proven developers, we pioneer the integration of advanced Unmanned Aerial Systems (UAS). From tactical strike platforms to revolutionary, ultra-high-speed interceptor drones engineered to neutralize asymmetrical airborne threats and safeguard critical state infrastructure, we deploy tomorrow's battlefield solutions today.

Catharticus handles the entire transaction lifecycle with absolute precision—from international end-user compliance and state-level transit permits to multi-modal tactical logistics executed under tight operational deadlines.

Strategic Consulting & Intelligence-Driven Solutions
